Gait instructions

walk
WALK rhythm. Upright torso with a very small forward angle, head steady, unhurried bright short footsteps. The opposite arm and leg swing in the reverse phase. Keep elbows slightly bent. The boot settles from heel to whole sole and pushes off at the toe. At least one foot is in stance throughout the cycle. Minimal hop, no racing posture.

run
RUN rhythm. A light forward lean from hips, relaxed neck, low bent elbows and tailing hair. Hips compress during stance and recover in flight. In guide poses 4 and 8 both feet are briefly off the ground. The leading leg changes between rows and the opposite arms exchange positions, with a clear drive and recovery. Hands remain well below chest level, loosely closed. Match the newly designed compact stride.

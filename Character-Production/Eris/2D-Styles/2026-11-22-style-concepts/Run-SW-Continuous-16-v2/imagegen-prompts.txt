이리스 달리기 16프레임 원화

내장 ImageGen으로 다섯 그림체의 전신 구성 원화를 새로 만들었습니다. 첫 번째 참고 이미지는 각 그림체의 대기 원화이고, 두 번째는 Parts/arm-parts-template.png입니다.

어깨와 손목은 소매에 이어집니다. 먼쪽 팔의 가림은 3D 관절 깊이를 따라 조정했습니다. 첫 검토에서 양팔이 함께 앞으로 보이는 자세를 발견해 가림과 위치를 조정한 뒤 전체 사이클을 다시 렌더했습니다.

01-pixel-art
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: alpha bitmap source artwork for a 2D game running animation.
Treat image 1 as the exact reference for Iris's face, chibi body proportions, hood, pale-pink curls and outfit. Image 2 is a strict technical diagram for the pose and placement of six pieces. Draw a brand-new character parts sheet in this medium: Hand-pixelled 16-bit RPG sprites, stepped indigo outer pixels and restrained warm cream/coral clusters, consistent square pixels.
A 3-column by 2-row landscape sheet with one isolated piece in each cell. Fully transparent empty space. No labels, guides, extra pieces or marks. Leave generous clear alpha borders around each piece. Fixed overhead front-left three-quarter view with the face looking to screen left.
Top left: full hood, head, bright star eyes, entire hair and body ending at the flared dress hem. A slightly forward athletic jog posture. Two little white ruffled shoulder caps are sewn onto the body and are clearly visible below the hair, so a flesh overlap can be placed directly under them. Draw a complete navy-and-ivory dress with its two fixed gold button columns, gold hem and embroidered badges. No arms or legs on this piece.
Top middle (near left arm) and top right (far right arm): draw a complete anatomically connected jogging arm in each cell. Match the L-shape and exact start, elbow and fist direction of the diagram: from shoulder at top, the upper arm angles to the right as it descends, and the elbow bends to the left into a puffy ivory sleeve, then a compact, softly closed fist aimed toward lower left. The short peach skin tab at the top is hidden in the assembled torso's ruffle. Only a slim strip of skin is between shoulder and navy band. The navy band is at the upper arm, close to the attachment. Soft ivory fabric starts here and rounds all the way over the elbow into the forearm and wrist. The elbow contour is one solid rounded limb, with one stable blue flower and one gold stripe on each sleeve. Tiny wrist cuff overlaps the fist with no visible wrist gap. Fingers are curled together like a relaxed running hand; do not draw dangling or straight fingers. Do not put a floating ruffle, top shoulder pad, separate ball or white flower above the skin tab. The shoulder ruffle belongs to the body piece. The far arm is viewed from the same camera angle and hidden part of the body, never a mirrored or reversed view. Same width and proportions as the sleeves and hands in image 1.
Bottom left, bottom middle: left and right boot only. Ivory fur ring, solid chunky sole, gold buckle and navy strap. Toes aim diagonally left. Open fur cuff with space for attached lower leg. Never add bare skin to the boots.
Bottom right: once-bent bare leg starting above the knee and ending inside a boot cuff, gently rounded soft anatomical shape in the illustration medium. No foot, no cloth or standalone parts.
Keep the dress, collar, hood and ornaments exactly recognizable. Make the hand-to-sleeve and shoulder-to-sleeve transitions clean and natural.

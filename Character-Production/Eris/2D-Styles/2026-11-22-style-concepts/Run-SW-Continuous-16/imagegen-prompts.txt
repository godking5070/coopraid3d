이리스 달리기 16프레임 원화

내장 ImageGen으로 그림체별 원화 여섯 파츠를 새로 만들었습니다. 첫 번째 참고 이미지는 해당 그림체의 대기 원화이고, 두 번째는 ../Run-SW-Rig-16/Parts/parts-template.png입니다.
완성 시트는 가까운 어깨와 소매, 발목과 부츠 입구가 겹쳐 이어지도록 렌더했습니다. 초안 검토 후 다리 비율, 부츠 겹침, 반대쪽 팔의 가림을 조정해 전신 16장을 다시 렌더했습니다.

01-pixel-art
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: transparent bitmap drawing master for a game sprite animation.
Draw a NEW professional 2D rig source for Iris that will be reposed through a complete run cycle. Image 1 is the exact character and rendering style reference. Image 2 defines placement and pose of the six isolated parts. Use the cool navy dress, warm ivory hood, cream-and-rose curls, bright blue star eyes, gold flower clasp, blue flowers, six gold front buttons, gold embroidered hems, navy shoulder bands, ivory bell sleeves, and buckled fur boots of Image 1.
Medium: Restrained 16-bit RPG pixel art, with crisp stepped outlines and small hand-placed clusters, square pixels, no antialias, no smooth gradients. Use the real palette and sharp pixel-art character proportions of Image 1.
Canvas: landscape three columns and two rows, alpha outside the art. Each part is a complete drawing, isolated inside its own cell with generous empty space. Follow Image 2 placement exactly, especially the bent arm silhouettes. No labels or visible guides.
1 TOP LEFT: body from hood to dress hem only. Draw the entire head, hood, hair, bow collar, two small white shoulder caps, and the COMPLETE A-line dress with two intact gold button rows and embroidered hem. Show both shoulders, with intact white ruffles over the joints. Finish the skirt under the prospective arm overlay and give it a continuous hem. Exclude the arms, hands and legs. Slight forward running lean. The face looks towards screen-left and down a little as viewed from above in front three-quarter.
2 TOP MIDDLE: nearer LEFT arm only, made as ONE continuous piece. Shoulder cap starts at top, a short exposed upper arm travels diagonally down-right to the dark navy arm band; a bell sleeve bends back diagonally down-left to a small relaxed closed hand. Definite L-like bend at the elbow. Put one blue flower and its gold stripe on the bell sleeve. Draw the upper shoulder skin long enough to overlap below the white ruffle of part 1. Use soft forms that remain legible when foreshortened.
3 TOP RIGHT: same bend and costume for the farther RIGHT arm, also a continuous shoulder-to-hand drawing with a relaxed closed hand. The far arm is seen from the same fixed viewpoint. Reuse the exact design vocabulary of the near arm. Angle and silhouette follow the template.
4 BOTTOM LEFT: near left boot only, the original ivory fur cuff, sole, navy strap and gold buckle with toe pointing toward screen-left and down. The boot is open at the top of the fur ring.
5 BOTTOM MIDDLE: far right boot only, same individual boot with the buckle on the correct outer side, same viewing direction. No leg above either boot.
6 BOTTOM RIGHT: one cropped upper thigh and lower bare leg in warm blush skin tones. Slightly flexed knee, continuous contour from above knee to down to the ankle, no foot. Leftward facing 3/4.
The result is a single master sheet of just these six pieces. Preserve image 1's character identity and chibi proportions. True transparent alpha. No guides, shadow on ground, writing, color swatches, extra pieces or duplicate characters.

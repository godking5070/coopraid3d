Avoid doubled shoulder caps, isolated shoulder dots, choppy halo marks, accidental transparent seams and separate floating cuffs at the shoulder. Keep Iris on-model with the sky-blue star jewel hair flower, warm blonde-to-pink curls, bright eyes, navy gold-button coat, ivory floral trim and chunky gold-buckled boots. Exactly 4 columns by 4 rows ordered by time, full unobstructed characters contained in equally spaced cells with ample transparent margins on every side. Real transparent alpha background.

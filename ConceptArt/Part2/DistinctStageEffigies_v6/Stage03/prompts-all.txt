===== 01-effigy.prompt.txt =====
Use case: stylized-concept / design variation. ASTELLA Part II Stage03, Alchemist of Frozen Veins, NORMAL HUMANOID ritual effigy.
INPUT ROLE: selected original Stage03 image is a FAMILY / STYLE / HUMAN-PROPORTION reference, NOT a locked exact body to pose three times. Keep the original's delicate long seven-to-eight-head human anatomy, platinum clinical material and blank sealed medical head family, but actively DIFFERENTIATE head shell, chest shell and LARGE MERCURY ARTIFACTS according to the design below.
LATEST USER CORRECTION: The previous variants looked identical with only poses changed. This new design must have a meaningfully different APPEARANCE and secondary concept while remaining recognizably Stage03. MERCURY ARTIFACTS are visual protagonists: THREE TO FOUR FEW LARGE FUNCTIONAL UNITS, not tiny jewelry. They visibly join back/spinal/forearm silver veins and transfer pulse/healing/backflow. Not arbitrary decorations or a hundred tiny parts.
MANDATORY HUMAN BODY: small head and neck, human shoulders/ribcage, narrow waist, recognizable pelvis, TWO upper/lower arms/hands and TWO long thighs/shins/ankles/feet. If artifacts removed the body remains a slender complete person-shaped effigy. No giant chalice/casket/barrel body, short column legs, architecture torso or generic robot. Body's ceramic panels follow smooth human volumes, not realistic skin. NO natural face, eyes/pupils, nose/lips, scalp, white bald head, hair, gender emphasis or class costume.
Mercury inside INTACT SEALED glass is OPAQUE cold silver-gray BELOW a visible horizontal meniscus, EMPTY transparent glass ABOVE. It must not look like transparent blue water. Any small mercury bead is contained within a closed glass loop/seal, never freely flying blobs. Minimal tiny green pulse. Glass connects through supported interface collars; back devices fuse into skeletal spine/scapula channels instead of a loose backpack.
STYLE: original clean fine outline, broad matte platinum-white ceramic / cool silver / muted navy planes, few pale gold collars, soft 2-3-tone lavender cel shadows, illustrated glass. Not PBR/photo, wet realism, rust, grime or intricate little ornaments. Deliberately colder silver palette than other stages. Medical veil is RIGID SHELL, never hair.
COMPOSITION: ONE complete original humanoid, 1536x1024 landscape mild 3/4 perspective, at least five-percent framing margins around head/back apparatus/hands/feet and held tool. Natural ground contact, all limbs and attachments coherent, no part hidden by oversized props. Low-contrast cold glass alchemy chamber with floor mercury grooves and faint green healing circles, no text/UI/panels/other people. No music mechanism, winding key, hourglass, furnace, celestial ring or weapon.

DISTINCT DESIGN: CONCEPT A: MERCURY PULSE RELIQUARY.
Change head shell into a short WHITE MEDICAL VEIL with two squared layered side drape plates and a small flat silver brow cap; blank oval face plate recessed inside, no familiar gold forehead clasp. Chest shell is two smooth curved ceramic protective halves enclosing a SMALL SEALED GLASS CHALICE RELIQUARY confined to sternum/ribcage dimensions; opaque silver fills its bottom third, green pulse just above the liquid. Waist/pelvis stay slim and human.
ARTIFACT UNIT 1 is that visible sternum chalice; UNIT 2 is TWO forearm-length OVAL SEALED MERCURY RESERVOIRS mounted vertically on left/right scapulae in a single supported ceramic yoke, silver levels distinct and upper clear glass. They replace the original single U backloop. UNIT 3 is a pair of WRIST MERCURY BEAD CIRCUITS, each a thick CLOSED oval glass ring enclosing several small silver beads, fixed around wrist via one ivory saddle and directly joined to forearm silver veins. Rings are readable palm-sized devices, not bracelets/jewelry.
Both hands cradle the sternum relic without obscuring its glass chamber, elbows slightly away, quiet upright stance with long legs, one foot a half-step forward. Back reservoirs project visibly beyond each shoulder edge but do not dwarf body. Clearly different head/chest/major back apparatus from original while preserving its clinical effigy family.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: ASTELLA Part II Stage03, The Alchemist of Frozen Veins, NORMAL ritual effigy. Design an original non-personal MERCURY RELIQUARY HEALER.
INPUT ROLES: Both supplied images are actual Part I boss references for controlled contours, carved broad shell mass, functional hollow object-body construction and gentle lavender cel shading ONLY. Do NOT copy their faces, helmets, armor costumes, weapons, bell, number of legs or sheet layout.
USER CORRECTION: This stage must have its own cold, rounded, hollow medical-relic identity. It is NOT a variation of a dancing music doll. No diamond head, V-shaped chest, winged pelvis, long thin articulated dancer limbs, exposed gold ball-joint template or a generic faceless humanoid robot. Design its sacrifice and bloodless mercy into the shape itself.
A human-like ritual presence is suggested by a sealed head/upper body, two arms and two grounded lower limbs, but the body is an empty sanctified vessel/medical effigy, NOT a human wearing equipment. No natural face, eyes/pupils, lips, nose, white bald head, hair, gender emphasis or player outfit.

Delicate clean contour lines, 2-3 soft lavender cel shadow tones. Matte illustrated materials, not PBR/photography or grainy paint. Simple massive forms with clear joins.
LORE AND FUNCTION: Blood completely removed, empty veins filled with cold mercury. Large FEW CLOSED GLASS mercury loops are fused into BACK/SPINE and connect internally to palms. They must grow through the body's own back ribs/structure, never a detachable backpack. Beneath a visible HORIZONTAL meniscus the mercury is OPAQUE silver-gray; above it empty transparent glass, no blue water. Normal glass INTACT. One tiny green pulse suggests final healing/cleansing. No musical plates, winding keys, clock hands, chains, furnace or hourglass.
COMPOSITION: ONE full-body 3/4 effigy, landscape 1536x1024, mild perspective, whole silhouette/head/tubes/hands/feet inside frame with margins. Countable two arms and two lower supports, physical grounded contact and readable mechanism connections. Restrained low-contrast cold platinum alchemy chamber with floor mercury channels and faint distant green healing circles. No other figures, text, labels, UI, sheets or panels.

DESIGN: A BROAD ROUND SEATED RELIQUARY, with the mass of a hollow spherical communion vessel, not a separate person on a throne. Its belly/chest is a large clean circular dark cavity surrounded by a thick rounded ceramic rim, empty and bloodless. Two short thick arms unfold sideways from the rim and terminate in LARGE open stone healing palms. Two wide short bent column legs descend from the round pelvic base to broad half-disc feet, integrated into a low crescent sitting support. A small oblong sealed MEDICAL VEIL HOOD grows from the top rim: a smooth layered ceramic canopy with an entirely dark blank recessed surface, no face or eye slit. Two thick low mercury arches curve behind the sphere and fuse into its rear ribs and palm conduits; a silver cistern fills the lower half of the backbody. Slight asymmetry, one palm closer and one lower, quiet sacrificial mercy. Round main mass and short thick limbs; no spindly marionette design.

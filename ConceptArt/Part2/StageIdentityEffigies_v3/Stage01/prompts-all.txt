Warm gold sand flows downward INSIDE only; no broken glass or outside leaking sand. Sand/death/memory is the body's function, NOT decorative props stuck to a person. No music bars, keys, mercury, fire or giant celestial rings.

Old damage as a few broad seams only.

Low-contrast round glass arena with three broad concentric rings, a few pale pylons and distant gold sand falls. No other people, UI, text, labels, turnaround or inset panels.

Wide flat knee-free pedestal legs, asymmetric weight shift but no dancing pose. One modest rigid survey bracket is embedded in the upper rear, almost flush. No shield, horns, cape or warrior anatomy.

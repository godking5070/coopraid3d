PRIMARY CORRECTION: Each stage must have its OWN ceremonial EFFIGY anatomy derived from that stage's story. The user rejected putting different props on the SAME slender music-dancer robot. Do not use a diamond/hexagon cap head, V-shaped breastplate, pointed hip flaps, skinny waist, paired rod limbs and round gold joints as a universal mannequin template. This is a new body architecture, not a reskin.
References are ONLY actual Part I drawings, for broad sculpted surfaces, fixed-material body construction, thin controlled lines and soft lavender 2-3 tone CEL illustration. Do NOT copy their sheet layout, recognizable face, haircut, clothes, literal boss identity or weapon. No previous Part II effigy is a reference.
Ceremonial effigy means an inhuman ritual body expressing a sacrifice: a stone/ceramic sealed idol, hollow reliquary, or living carved vessel, not a handsome/pretty human adventurer with gadgets. No real human face, pupils, lips, natural hair, bald human head, gender emphasis or playable-class outfit. A blank mask or blind enclosure may be shaped SPECIFICALLY for this function. Gesture and shell mass convey ritual. Use few large attached components and readable joints, stable support and deliberate negative spaces, not ornament noise. No PBR, real skin, gritty paint, rust or brown grime.
ONE full-body single subject, landscape 1536x1024, mild three-quarter perspective, minimum 5% clear margin around whole silhouette including all head/hand/foot/large apparatus and weapon tips. Planted contacts and coherent rigid axes. Background tailored to this stage and low contrast. No text, labels, panels, alternate views, UI, other people, T-pose or schematic.

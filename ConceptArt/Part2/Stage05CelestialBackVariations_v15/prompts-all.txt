Asset: ASTELLA Part2 Stage05, one FULL-BODY celestial back-artifact variation at1536x1024. This is one of three requested concepts.
The sole input is the EXACT USER-LIKED BODY/POSE reference MajesticStage02to06_v9/Stage05/01-boss.png. User likes its physique and original planet atmosphere, but dislikes the rear apparatus and particularly its connection to the spear. They ask for CELESTIAL motifs rather than a literal planet, and allow a CLOAK.
KEEP THE BODY EXACTLY: same tall strong humanoid proportions, closed pointed navy/ivory mask, star-cracked ivory cuirass and original shoulder/arm armor, narrow waist, original hip/thigh/shin shells, hands/feet. Same upright torso, same bent forward leg and braced rear leg with foot positions, same elbows and TWO HANDS gripping the diagonal spear at the SAME locations. Same armor color/pattern, no hunching, new torso pistons, altered mass, face/hair/personality or new costume covering front. Two arms/two long legs, five articulated digits per hand.
WEAPON MUST BE INDEPENDENT:
The original straight two-ended HOUR-HAND SPEAR stays the same length/angle/position and is HELD BY THE TWO HANDS ONLY. Rear end has small armillary hoop and star finial at viewer-upper-left; broad forward point with round star aperture remains near floor viewer-lower-right. Keep both ends complete.
REMOVE the LARGE BRASS CLAMP/RING/FORK that mechanically grips the SHAFT NEAR THE WAIST. Remove ALL back-to-spear rods, long diagonal support shafts, pelvic pivots touching the spear, linking bars/struts/tubes/chains. The slim spear shaft should run UNINTERRUPTED in front of the waist. A small burgundy pennant tied directly to shaft can remain, with a tiny ordinary tie collar only. There must be NO rigid bridge between weapon and torso/back relic, no spear anchored to pelvis, no back relic mounted on weapon. Back components attach ONLY at upper back/shoulders via short unobtrusive separate mounts. At the waist crossing, make spear visibly in front with air clearance and subtle separation shadow, no touching mount.
REMOVE the old filled navy star-map shield, irregular ivory rim, full planet, huge meridian clock frame and ALL old rear support poles. Replace ONLY the rear silhouette with the new celestial concept described below. The back element is the sole major creative change; minimal shaft-clamp deletion is also required. Do not restore a literal sphere/globe or overhanging roof.
STYLE: exactly reference's polished stylized fantasy2D game concept illustration, precise clean outlines, broad smooth ivory/navy/brass forms, restrained lavender2-3tone cel shadows, readable finite edges. Astronomical mood from a few strong star/constellation/celestial curves, not photographs of galaxies or noisy particles. Keep faceless ceremonial artifact identity; a cloak is an integrated celestial relic and not a new human hero costume.
COMPOSITION: same original mild front3/4 view, camera and full-body frame. Head/hands/feet/back tips/hem/spear ends all inside1536x1024 with clear60-80px margins. Body remains in original position and size. New back attachment should be fully visible beside/behind shoulders and flanks without obscuring head/chest, either arm, hand, or both leg outlines. No floor-length material hiding feet, no moving the pose to show new device. Background remains the same lower-contrast obsidian star-chart floor and lavender cosmic observatory arches. No added people, letters, UI, watermark, collage or inset.
Strict final priority: keep exact original BODY/ARMOR/POSE/SPEAR identity; absolutely NO mechanical connection between BACK and SPEAR; change only back motif according to this option.

BACK CONCEPT1 — CONSTELLATION ATLAS CLOAK.
A substantial flowing DEEP NAVY CLOAK attaches to TWO SMALL BRASS FASTENERS behind the existing shoulder armor, with no front clasp and no change to pauldrons. The cloak spreads behind the shoulders and drapes downward in THREE BROAD HEAVY FOLDS, opening in a shallow fan beside both flanks; hem ends at mid-calf and remains above floor/feet. It is NOT a globe, metal shield, celestial dome or halo.
The outer textile has just THREE LARGE elegant golden CONSTELLATION PATHS, thin brass-gold arcs and6-8large four-point star nodes, distributed across broad smooth fabric planes. Subtle muted indigo/lavender inner lining, thick restrained ivory-gold edge binding. A small stepped break at bottom edges suggests a worn sacred celestial atlas, not shredded confetti or ribbons. Few generous graceful folds with physical cloth weight and clear attachment points; no cape over face/helmet/front chest or arms. No royal fur, embroidery noise, crown, floor-length train or expanding windstorm.
The cloak can billow moderately at the rear side to show its star chart and leave both legs readable. It should suggest a sealed fragment of the NIGHT SKY draped from the back of an artifact guardian. The spear has no support connection of ANY kind: remove waist clamp and old long bar completely; hands alone carry it. All celestial decoration remains on the cloak behind body, never a ring around spear.
